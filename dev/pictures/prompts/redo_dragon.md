# Jump 'n' Mine: the dragon, consistent frames

I'm making Jump 'n' Mine, a 2D side-view game for children. A chubby, mischievous purple dragon flies in above the mine and spits fire down.

The attached dragon frames are almost right, but **frames 2 and 3 (wings down) are drawn smaller than frames 1 and 4**, so the dragon would shrink and grow while it flaps. Please redraw the **sheet of 4 frames with the dragon at exactly the same size and position in every frame**: the same body, head and tail outline, only the wings, the mouth and the fire change. Facing **right**, seen from the side while flying:

1. wings up
2. wings down
3. about to spit: cheeks puffed, mouth glowing orange
4. spitting a burst of fire **downwards**

**One sheet 3200 x 600 px, 4 equal cells of 800 x 600 px, fully transparent background (real alpha).** Let the dragon fill most of its cell (about 600 px wide), not a small figure in a big empty cell. Keep the colors: purple #8a3fd0 body, orange-yellow #f0b04f belly, small horns, big friendly eyes, bat wings.

The fire sheet (`fire_sheet.png`) is fine; keep the dragon's fire in the same style.

Style: the same bright, friendly, hand-painted cartoon look as the attached references (bold dark outlines, soft shading with light from the top left, chunky readable shapes). No text, no watermark, no drop shadow outside the sprites. The game is for children of 6-10 and is seen from 2-3 meters away, so keep the shapes bold and clear.
