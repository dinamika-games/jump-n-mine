# Jump 'n' Mine: the title painting

I'm making Jump 'n' Mine, a 2D side-view game for children of 6-10, played by jumping in front of a webcam: each child is a dwarf who digs **down** through a mine with a pickaxe.

Redraw the title painting `reference_current_title.png` at **1920 x 1080 px** with these changes, keeping everything else (the layout, the sky, the grass, the four dwarves, the dragon, the glowing chest):

- **No bombs.** Replace the red crate with the bomb by a block of earth with a **glowing green gas pocket**, like the attached `gassy_rock.png` (or `gassy_rock_activated.png`, the gas seeping out).
- **The water spring** on the left is a coil spring now; make it a **water pipe in the rock wall gushing clear blue water**, like the attached `spring_open.png`.
- The dwarves must look like the attached `reference_blue_dwarf.png` (grey steel helmets with a yellow lamp, big cream beards), one each in a **red #e04848, blue #3f8ee8, green #3fbf5a and yellow #f2b630** tunic. They dig **down** (pickaxes swinging at the blocks under their feet) and jump; nobody hits sideways.
- The dragon should look like `reference_dragon.png` (chubby, purple, friendly).
- Use the blocks of `blocks_6x3_320px.png` (top row, The Copper Hills), the sky of `copper_hills_sky_1920x400.png` and the cave of `copper_hills_cave_background_1280x960.png`, and the gems and the chest of `items.png`.
- Leave the **top third calm** (sky) for the title, and keep the **middle and bottom third a little darker and calmer**, since big menu cards will be laid over them. No text.

Style: the same bright, friendly, hand-painted cartoon look as the attached references (bold dark outlines, soft shading with light from the top left, chunky readable shapes). No text, no watermark, no drop shadow outside the sprites. The game is for children of 6-10 and is seen from 2-3 meters away, so keep the shapes bold and clear.
