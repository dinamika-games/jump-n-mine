# Jump 'n' Mine: the four dwarves, consistent

I'm making Jump 'n' Mine, a 2D side-view game where children are dwarves who dig down a mine by jumping in front of a webcam.

The attached `reference_blue_dwarf_GOOD.png` is exactly right: the style, the proportions, the 4 x 3 grid of poses and the **real transparent background**. Please make **three more sheets that match it exactly**, the same dwarf, the same 12 poses in the same cells, at the same size and position in each cell, only the tunic color changes:

1. **red** tunic #e04848
2. **green** tunic #3fbf5a
3. **yellow** tunic #f2b630

The poses, row by row, as in the blue sheet: (1) idle, pickaxe on the shoulder; (2) walking, one foot forward; (3) walking, the other foot forward; (4) pickaxe raised high; (5) mid-swing; (6) the pickaxe hitting **down** at the ground in front of the feet; (7) a second hitting-down frame (the pickaxe deeper, chips flying); (8) dizzy, X eyes, three stars circling, a little sooty; (9) wading under water with bubbles; (10) holding a golden sparkling pickaxe; (11) inside a glowing blue protective bubble; (12) with green springy boots.

Each sheet: **1448 x 1086 px, a 4 x 3 grid of equal cells, fully transparent background (real alpha, not a painted checkerboard or grey backdrop)**, the dwarf facing **right**, feet on the same baseline in every cell.

Do **not** copy from `reference_four_colors_BAKED_BACKGROUND.png`: its grey checkered background is painted in. The two red references differ a little from each other; follow the blue sheet instead.

Style: the same bright, friendly, hand-painted cartoon look as the attached references (bold dark outlines, soft shading with light from the top left, chunky readable shapes). No text, no watermark, no drop shadow outside the sprites. The game is for children of 6-10 and is seen from 2-3 meters away, so keep the shapes bold and clear.
